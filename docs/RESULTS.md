## Findings

1. **Winter is the weak season for road service.** 48.1% of road cases met the
   60-minute target in December to February, against 66.0% in the rest of the
   year (median arrival 62 vs 51 min). Battery cases peak at the same
   time, so winter staffing and battery-only service vehicles are the first lever to look at.
2. **Regions differ a lot.** Bavaria has the lowest SLA compliance (50.8%),
   Berlin the highest (76.1%). The slower regions are the more rural
   ones, so a single national target hides where members wait longest.
3. **Waiting time costs satisfaction.** Members whose help arrived within the target rated the service
   4.58/5 on average, against 4.07/5 when it did not.
4. **The digital intake works.** The app share rose from 17.6% of cases before July to
   34.0% after the launch, and the average time until a dispatcher confirmed an app case fell
   from 5.0 to 2.4 minutes.
